Services

An exit shaped around your plans.

Some owners want a clean break. Others want to stay on for a few years or keep a stake. We structure each deal around what you want next.

Ways we work together

Four routes
to a well-planned exit

Full acquisition

A clean break, on your timeline

We buy 100% of the shares. You receive your proceeds at completion, with an agreed handover period so customers and staff experience a smooth transition.

Best for

Owners ready to retire or move on within the next year.

Majority investment

Take value now, share the upside

We acquire a controlling stake while you keep a share and stay involved, so you benefit from the growth that follows.

Best for

Owners who want to stay involved for a few more years.

Succession planning

Plan the exit before you need it

We help you plan a phased exit over one to three years, including building a management team that can lead without you.

Best for

Owners who are one to three years from stepping back.

Management transition

Back the leaders you've built

We work alongside your existing managers, support promising internal leaders, and add operational and financial help where the business needs it.

Best for

Businesses with a strong second tier of managers.

Our process

From first call
to completion

Every deal is different, but most follow the same six stages. You'll always know where you are.

  1. Introductory call

    A confidential conversation about your business, your goals and your timeline.

    • No documents needed
    • No obligation
    • Fully confidential

  2. NDA & information

    We sign a non-disclosure agreement and review your headline financials.

    • Last three years of accounts
    • Current management figures
    • Customer and team overview

  3. Indicative offer

    We set out a valuation and deal structure in Heads of Terms, and explain how we reached each figure.

    • Valuation and rationale
    • Deal structure
    • Proposed timeline

  4. Due diligence

    A focused review of the business, with requests kept proportionate and coordinated with your advisers.

    • Financial and tax
    • Legal and contracts
    • Commercial and HR

  5. Completion

    We agree the Share Purchase Agreement, sign and transfer funds.

    • Share Purchase Agreement
    • Funds transferred
    • Staff told your way

  6. Handover & growth

    We follow the transition plan we agreed together, then invest in the company's next stage.

    • Agreed handover period
    • Team kept together
    • Long-term investment
FAQ

Questions
owners ask us

Will my employees keep their jobs?

Yes. We buy businesses for their people and reputation. Employees transfer with their existing terms, and keeping the team together is part of every plan we make.

Do I need an adviser or broker?

No, you can approach us directly. Many owners do appoint a corporate finance adviser and solicitor, and we're happy to work with them.

How do you value a business?

We look mainly at sustainable profit, the quality and predictability of revenue, customer concentration and how dependent the business is on you as owner. We explain our reasoning openly.

Will my customers find out I'm talking to you?

Not unless you decide to tell them. Conversations are confidential from the first call and covered by an NDA once we exchange information.

Can I stay involved after the sale?

Yes, if you'd like to. Some owners stay on as a director or consultant for a period. Others prefer a clean handover. We agree this with you before Heads of Terms.
