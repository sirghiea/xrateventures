For business owners

Selling shouldn't mean closing down

Many owners reach the point where they want to retire, reduce their hours or move on to something new. Too often the options look narrow: wind the company down, pass it to a family member who doesn't want it, or sell to a buyer who strips it for parts.

We offer a different route. We acquire the business, keep the team and the name, and invest in the next stage of growth. You get a fair price and a clear plan, and your staff and customers keep the company they rely on.

Led by a founder, not a fund

Meet Alex Sirghie
Founder & Group Chairman

Alex trained as a ceramicist, taught himself to code and spent more than ten years in technology, building AI systems for companies like EY, Betfair and IBM.

Now he buys established UK businesses and brings that experience inside: modern systems, better data and practical AI that frees people up for the work that matters.
