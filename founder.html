My story

Why I started
XrateVentures

I've spent more than ten years in technology, most recently building AI systems for businesses. I've seen how much a few well-built tools can change a company's week.

In company after company I saw the same pattern. Demand was rarely the problem. Manual work, slow follow-up and old systems were.

XrateVentures is the next step. Instead of advising businesses from the outside, I buy them and make the changes from the inside, for the long term.

What I believe

How I think
about growing a business

"Don't sell AI. Sell productivity."

Technology only matters if it saves time, cuts errors or helps people do better work. That's how I judge every change I make.

"Growth dies by a thousand cuts."

Slow responses, manual handoffs, leads left sitting in spreadsheets. I look for the small leaks first, because fixing them adds up fast.

"Respect what's already working."

The team, the name and the customer relationships took years to build. My job is to give them better tools, never to start again from scratch.

My approach

The first 100 days
after completion

Change happens carefully and in the right order. This is how I approach a new acquisition.

  1. Listen and learn
    Days 1–30

    I spend time with the team, customers and suppliers to understand how the business really works before changing anything.

    • Meet every team member
    • Talk to key customers
    • Review systems and processes

  2. Quick wins
    Days 30–60

    We fix the small frustrations that slow people down every day, which builds trust and momentum.

    • Remove manual double entry
    • Replace unreliable tools
    • First simple automations

  3. Growth roadmap
    Days 60–100

    Together with the management team, I set out a clear plan for technology, operations and revenue growth.

    • Technology roadmap
    • Revenue targets
    • Investment priorities

  4. Long-term growth
    Beyond day 100

    We deliver the roadmap step by step, measure the results and keep investing in the business.

    • Quarterly reviews
    • Ongoing investment
    • Team development
