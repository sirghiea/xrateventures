My story

Built in tech.
Focused on real businesses.

I've spent more than a decade working in technology, most recently in artificial intelligence. I've seen first-hand how the right tools change the way a company works: fewer manual tasks, better information and faster decisions.

I've also seen that many of the UK's best businesses haven't had the chance to benefit. They run on spreadsheets, paper files and systems that were installed years ago. Their owners have been busy serving customers and running the company, so modernisation keeps slipping down the list.

That gap is the opportunity. A business with loyal customers, a skilled team and a strong reputation already has the hardest parts in place. Add modern technology and fresh thinking, and it can grow faster than it ever has.

That's why I started XrateVentures. I buy companies from owners who are ready to step back, keep what makes them special, and use my experience to take them to the next level.

My approach

The first 100 days
after completion

Change happens carefully and in the right order. This is how I typically approach a new acquisition.

  1. Listen and learn
    Days 1–30

    I spend time with the team, customers and suppliers to understand how the business really works before changing anything.

    • Meet every team member
    • Talk to key customers
    • Review systems and processes

  2. Quick wins
    Days 30–60

    We fix the small frustrations that slow people down every day, which builds trust and momentum.

    • Remove manual double entry
    • Replace unreliable tools
    • First simple automations

  3. Growth roadmap
    Days 60–100

    Together with the management team, I set out a clear plan for technology, operations and revenue growth.

    • Technology roadmap
    • Revenue targets
    • Investment priorities

  4. Long-term growth
    Beyond day 100

    We deliver the roadmap step by step, measure the results and keep investing in the business.

    • Quarterly reviews
    • Ongoing investment
    • Team development
