About us

The future is built on what you've created.

For decades, owners across the UK have built companies that serve their communities, create jobs and shape whole industries. Yet when it's time to step back, too many believe the only choices are to close down or hand over to someone who doesn't share their vision.

Who we are

A successor
you can trust

Selling your business should mean that what you built keeps thriving after you leave. XrateVentures gives owners a structured, strategic exit that protects their legacy, their employees and their values.

We focus on B2B, contract-based and professional service companies with an established reputation and recurring revenue. These are businesses where people and relationships matter, and where the wrong buyer can do lasting damage.

Our commitment: we never strip a business for short-term gain. We invest in its future, so it's stronger in ten years than on the day we bought it.

Our outlook

A UK-wide approach
with a long-term vision

Small and medium-sized businesses employ more than half of the UK's private-sector workforce. Many of their owners are nearing retirement with no succession plan in place.

When those businesses close or are sold to the wrong buyer, local jobs, supplier relationships and decades of know-how go with them. We want to keep that value in the towns and cities where it was built.

We work with owners across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Wherever your business is based, our commitment is the same: a fair exit for you and a secure future for the people who depend on the company.

A note from our founder

"I'm inspired by business leaders who reinvest in the places that made them. XrateVentures is my contribution: helping owners get the exit they deserve, while their businesses stay a driving force in the UK economy."

Alex Sirghie, Founder & Group Chairman
